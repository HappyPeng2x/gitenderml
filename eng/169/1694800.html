お構かまいなし
  • n
      • having no thought for
      • having no consideration for
      • having no regard for
      • not caring about
      彼かれは他人たにんの感情かんじょうなど全くまったくおかまいなしだ。
      He pays no attention to others' feelings.
  • n
      • disregarding
      • overlooking
  • n
    arch
      • being found not guilty (in an Edo-period court)
      • acquittal

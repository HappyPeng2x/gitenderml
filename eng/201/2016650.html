殉じゅんじる
  • v1vi
      • to sacrifice oneself (for)
      • to give one's life (for)
      • to die for (for)
  • v1vi
      • to follow (someone) in taking the same action (out of respect or loyalty; e.g. resigning together with a superior)
      • to follow suit (out of a sense of duty)
  • v1vi
      • to follow (one's master) into the grave
      • to commit suicide (after one's lord has died)

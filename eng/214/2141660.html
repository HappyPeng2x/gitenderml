八はっ寸すん
  • n
      • distance of eight sun (approx. 24 cm)
  • n
      • dish or tray of this size (esp. used in kaiseki cuisine to serve several kinds of delicacies)
      • food served in such a dish
  • n
      • variety of thick, traditional Japanese paper

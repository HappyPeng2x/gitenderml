向むいている
  • expv1
      • to be cut out for (e.g. a job)
      • to be suited (to)
      アンタ本当ほんとうに器用きようねっ。主夫しゅふとか向いむいてるんじゃない？
      You're really handy, aren't you? Don't you think you'd make a good house-husband?
